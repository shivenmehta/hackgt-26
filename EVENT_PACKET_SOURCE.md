### **NSA**
🔉 **Challenge 1: HEARSAY: The Audio Authentication Challenge**
<span underline="true">Scenario: </span>
Welcome to your first assignment with the National Security Agency’s Multimedia Forensics and Authentication Team.  The stakes have never been higher. Your adversaries are already deploying synthetic-voice systems at scale. The question isn’t whether the fake call will come, it is whether we’ll know it’s fake when it does.
<span underline="true">Your mission: </span>
Build an AI system that can autonomously analyze an audio file, determine the likelihood score it has been synthesized, and the type of manipulation. The system should take any audio file as input (WAV, MP3, M4A, etc...), automatically orchestrate multiple forensic analysis techniques, output a synthetic likelihood score (0-100%), and optionally identify the type of manipulation. A labeled training set and held out test set will be provided. Submit a CSV file with the predicted results on the test set for evaluation. Cloud services, open-source LLMs, speech models, and signal-processing toolkits are all fair game. How you combine them is up to you!
<span color="blue">**Prizes:**</span> 
- 1st Place: Sonos Era 100 Alexa Speaker
- 2nd Place: Audio-Technica AT2040 USB Mic
- 3rd Place: JBL Flip 5 Bluetooth Speaker
<empty-block/>
📃 **Challenge 2: Packet Pursuit**
<span underline="true">Scenario</span>: 
The National Security Agency has intercepted network traffic from a notorious group of bad actors who are actively planning an attack aimed at disrupting critical infrastructure around the nation. The traffic contains critical information about the attack, and it is up to you to analyze the traffic, discover the hidden information, and reverse engineer the secret file. The security of our nation – and the success of our mission – depends on your expertise and determination.
<span underline="true">Your Mission</span>:
- Analyze a packet capture containing the bad actors’ network traffic.
- The challenge has five parts that build on each other. For each part, a hidden flag will need to be uncovered and submitted as an answer.
- Points are awarded for each flag that is discovered. A tie will be broken based on the team that gets to the point value first.
<span color="blue">**Prizes:**</span> 
- 1st Place: XBOX Series X 1TB Console with XBOX Wireless Controller
- 2nd Place: XBOX Bluetooth Wireless Gaming Over-the-Ear Headset
<empty-block/>
💻 **Challenge 3: Codebreaker**
The NSA Codebreaker Challenge provides students with a hands-on opportunity to develop their reverse-engineering / low-level code analysis skills while working on a realistic problem set centered around the NSA's mission. Reverse engineering is a crucial skill for those involved in the fight against malware, advanced persistent threats, and similar malicious cyber activities. As the organization tasked with protecting U.S. government national security information systems, NSA is looking to develop these skills in university students and prospective future employees.
NSA’s Codebreaker Challenge is expected to launch September 24, 2026 and students are invited to get a head start on opening weekend during HackGT.

# FAQs

**When is HackGT 13?**

- HackGT 13: Seaside Museum is Sep 25, 2026 to Sep 26, 2026! Hacking will start at 8:00pm on Friday, September 25th and end at 8:00am on Sunday, September 27th.
  **When is opening/judging/events/etc?**
- The full schedule is out on our website!
  **What happens at a hackathon?**
- Hackathons are a place to attend workshops, go to events, meet new people, and hack! While you are working on your project, you will also have the opportunity to destress with events and hone your skills with workshops hosted by various organizations. You will also have the opportunity to interact with sponsors. Of course, you also make and submit a project for a chance to win prizes!
  **When are meals?**
- Meal times and details will be on our [live site](http://live.hexlabs.org) and announced over Discord!
  **How do I report issues?**
- The help desk will be available to assist you! Reach out on Discord or in-person!
- For emergencies please contact GTPD (404-894-2500)
  **What if I do not finish my project?**
- Unless you are a travel reimbursement participant, you are not required to finish your project or demo at Expo! However, if you do choose to submit, you will present your project to judges, so try to have a working product. Hackathon projects aren’t perfect, but it may be better to have a working project with limited features rather than one that doesn’t work!
  **How much experience do I need?**
- Participants of all skill levels are welcome! Everyone is here to support you and help you create a project you’re proud of!
  **How do I find teammates?**
- You can use [Match](https://match.hexlabs.org/), our online team formation platform to search for teammates! Also, after opening ceremonies, there will be a mixer where you will be able to interact with other participants and discuss project ideas!  Do not be discouraged if you do not have a team yet!
  **Can I change teammates?**
- Teammate decisions are left up to participants. The team that you will be judged with is the one that is listed on the submission.
  **How do travel reimbursements work?**
- If you are a travel reimbursement participant, you are required to present at Expo in order to receive a reimbursement. Those who present will be contacted after the event with specific reimbursement instructions!
  **Prize shipment?**
- Prizes will not be shipped, and prize winners are expected to pick up their prizes in-person after closing ceremonies or authorize a teammate to pick it up for them.
  **Theme of project?**
- You are not required to theme your project in any specific way, but we do have tracks that you can align with to win prizes!
  **What is Devpost?**
- Devpost is a website for participants to submit their project for judging. This involves a video commentary highlighting the details of the project and a small write-up involving the inspiration, challenges, and method behind the project.
  **I have more questions!**
- Please contact us at hello@hexlabs.org
