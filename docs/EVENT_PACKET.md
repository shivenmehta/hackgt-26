## Submission constraints

- Maximum four people per team.
- Enter one track and multiple sponsor challenges if all relevant requirements are met.
- Four listed tracks: Oracle of the Deep (ML/AI and visualization), The Lighthouse Laboratory (AR/VR/XR), The Shipyard (hardware), and A Marina's Mission presented by Aramco (social good).
- The FAQ describes Devpost submission with video commentary and a short write-up covering inspiration, challenges, and method. An exact separate Devpost deadline and general judging rubric were not established in the retrieved text.
- Travel reimbursement participants must present at Expo.
- Prizes require in-person pickup after closing ceremonies, personally or through an authorized teammate; they are not shipped.

## A Marina's Mission — chosen event track

Presented by Aramco Americas, this is the social-good track. Its brief asks
participants to build technology that makes a real difference in areas such
as health, climate, energy, education, or a personally relevant problem.
The retrieved description does not prescribe a particular technology or give
a separate detailed judging rubric.

The team selected this track on September 25, 2026. Our intended connection is
helping people find nutritious food options within their budgets. Reducing
unused ingredients is a possible additional benefit, not an established result.
See `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md` for team plans, separate from the event requirements.

The listed track prizes are Bose QC 2nd Gen Headphones for first place and a
Ninja CREAMi Ice Cream Maker for second place, per winning team member (up to four).

## Visa — Reimagine Shopping with Generative AI

This is the team's selected sponsor challenge, in addition to Marina's Mission.

Build a generative-AI-powered commerce experience that makes shopping intuitive, personalized, and frictionless, enables secure and trusted payments, and transforms one or more shopping stages: discovery, decision-making, personalization, checkout, payments, loyalty/rewards, or post-purchase experiences.

Prize: $5,000 for the top winning team. This section does not specify a mandatory Visa API, live-payment requirement, separate deliverables, or judging rubric. Confirm implementation expectations with the sponsor.

Team interpretation, not event rules: the proposed budget-aware food planner could address discovery, comparison, personalization, and budget management. Meaningful generative AI and a credible payment experience should address the brief. Optimization/gradient descent alone does not establish generative AI. Do not describe mocked payments as live integration.

## Meta — Bringing People Closer Together with AI

- Build an AI-powered social product that strengthens human connection, with meaningful AI use.
- Deliver a working prototype, a 2–3 minute demo video, a public code repository, and a short write-up explaining the audience, how connections are strengthened, and why AI is essential.
- Judging: meaningful human connection, essential and well-integrated AI, originality, and working-demo strength.
- Top three teams receive an invitation to a second-round hackathon at Meta's Menlo Park campus, travel/accommodations, and swag.

Team interpretation: a generic social feed alone would not establish these requirements. Social functionality remains an optional team idea.
